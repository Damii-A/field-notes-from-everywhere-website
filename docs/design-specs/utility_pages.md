# FNFE Utility Pages Experience Spec
Scope: Contact, Privacy & Cookies, Terms, and Disclosures. This document defines the content and experience requirements for FNFE's secondary public utility pages.
## Section 1 — Contact
### 1.1 On-Page Content
HeadingWant to get in touch?
TextHave a question, suggestion, or just want to say hello? We'd love to hear from you.
Email address[contact email address]
### 1.2 Experience & Layout
The page is intentionally minimal: heading, short text, and the general FNFE contact email address only.
The full Contact composition is centered horizontally and vertically within the main page area.
The email address should be clickable.
The page inherits the standard public FNFE header and global footer.
There is no contact form, enquiry categorization, or additional Contact-page sections.
## Section 2 — Shared Legal & Disclosure Page Template
Applies to: Privacy & Cookies, Terms, and Disclosures.
### 2.1 Page Content
Privacy & CookiesCMS-managed body content.
TermsCMS-managed body content.
DisclosuresCMS-managed body content.
The actual body copy for these pages is intentionally not defined in this experience specification. Each page should provide a simple content container that can be populated and updated through the CMS.
### 2.2 Shared Experience & Design Requirements
All three pages inherit the standard public FNFE header and global footer.
They inherit the established FNFE public-site design system, including typography, colors, spacing, link styling, and other shared visual conventions.
Each page displays its page title followed by its CMS-managed body content.
The body should use a restrained, readable long-form content treatment appropriate for legal and disclosure information.
These pages do not require bespoke editorial layouts or distinct visual identities.
They should not introduce unique animations, promotional modules, Reading Room product elements, or other custom experiences.
Claude Design may determine the exact content width, typography scale, spacing, and responsive treatment within the established FNFE design system.
